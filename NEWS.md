# spatialfamily 0.2.0

New analyses: Spatial Panel Regression (unit / time / two-way fixed effects SAR, SEM, SDM, SDEM, SLX), Geographically Weighted Regression, Industrial Agglomeration
(location quotients, locational Gini, Ellison-Glaeser, Herfindahl, Krugman), Gravity Model (PPML with high-dimensional fixed effects and multiway clustering) and
Spatial DID (distance rings, spatially lagged treatment, Conley standard errors). Examples: Columbus panel, region-industry employment, bilateral trade, spillover DID.
Validated against splm, GWmodel, fixest and Stata 19 (spregress, spxtregress, ppmlhdfe, reghdfe).

# spatialfamily 0.1.0
